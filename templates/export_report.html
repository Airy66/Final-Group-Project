Methodology and limitations

Only server-validated records frozen into this analysis are used across metrics, charts and exports. Analytics dashboard filters are validated against that frozen set and recalculated by the server. A platform price comparison is qualified only when at least two marketplaces are represented within one product configuration and one condition; the platform median is used to reduce the effect of a single unusually low listing. Marketplace prices, availability and seller information may change after collection. Review source evidence before making a decision.
